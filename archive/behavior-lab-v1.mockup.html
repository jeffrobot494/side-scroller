How this maps to the plan

  • Slice 1 MVP = the arena on a generated multi-elevation level, the transport bar, the rosters, and the three overlays: LOS line, sense.* readout, utility scoreboard (+ commitment).
  • The scoreboard is the only piece needing runtime work — tickUtility in src/mission/enemyspec/brain.js already computes every score; stash the last breakdown on root.brainState and read it here.
  • Both rosters are spec agents differing by team ("player" / "enemy"); blue runs through updateCompanionSpec, red through updateSpecEnemy.
  • Numeric levers land in the config SCHEMA so the Settings tab generates them too.
  • Dashed panels are deliberately not Slice 1: metrics + replay arrive with Slice 5, the ghost player with Slice 4, presets whenever they earn their keep.
