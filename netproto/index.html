Red diamonds are fliers: 30 HP, they patrol and bob, and they shoot back. Their rounds are the red ones — and platforms stop them, so cover works.

What you're looking at

Every rectangle on screen, including yours, is drawn exactly where the last snapshot put it. No prediction, no reconciliation, no interpolation. The orange crosshair is the only thing that moves without asking the server — the gap between it and your aim line is the round trip.

The fliers are the harder test. Dodging a shot you can see coming is where predicting nothing costs the most, because the dodge does not begin until the server has heard about it.
