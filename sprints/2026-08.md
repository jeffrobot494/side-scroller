## The premise

Sometime in the last hundred years, lizard people reached back and altered
Earth's timeline, making humanity their unwitting and obedient servants. You are
the secret organisation that detected it. Your ops officer identifies *points of
intervention*; you travel there and stop them, restoring the timeline one thread
at a time. The lizards detect incursions and meet you when you arrive.

Missions have a *when* as well as a *where*. Lose one and the intervention
stands — later missions take place in the world that failure created.

The time machine is a rough prototype: energy-hungry and imprecise. What that
costs the player — arriving in the wrong decade, having to find the energy home,
or a warp bubble on a timer — is task 12's design work, not settled here.

The core loop is unchanged. Hub → lead → mission → results → advance day.

**Campaign structure precedes the story generator by dependency, not

**The shock absorber is 16–17.** Nothing else depends on them, so if the story

16 are Bo's alone; every implement task is buildable from a design doc by an
agent. Being blocked on a design task blocks everything downstream of it.
