Revision, not regeneration. The current sparse spec goes back to the model as the prior assistant turn, the instruction as the next user turn — the same shape generateEnemySpec's repair round already uses. A revision lands in a staging slot, never straight onto your work: you see what changed, the verdict, and keep or discard. History is a stack of specs, so Undo is free.
